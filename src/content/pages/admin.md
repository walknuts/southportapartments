## Definitions

Managing Southport requires understanding specific definitions and their relevance.

### Units plan

A legal group of units. Southport is registered as Units Plan No. 4368.

### Developer

A lessee who applies to develop a units plan on their land. Southport's developer was GEOCON.

### Owners corporation

A corporation of unit owners responsible for managing their units plan. Some Australian states call it a 'body corporate'.

### Strata

A units plan's finances, people, and legals --- described as the 'software'.

### Facilities

A units plan's buildings, equipment, and systems --- described as the 'hardware'.

### Unit 'class' (A or B)

Southport's units are 'class A' because:

- that is how they were registered (Sheet 1 of the registered Site Plan), s 10
- their 'boundaries [are] defined by reference to the floors, walls and ceilings of the building', s 18(1).

### Building class (1 to 10)

Southport's buildings are 'class 2' according to NCC Volume 1, Part A6, because they 'contain two or more sole-occupancy units', s A6G3(1).

The NCC defines sole-occupancy unit as:

> A room or other part of a building for occupation by one or joint owner, lessee, tenant, or other occupier to the exclusion of any other owner, lessee, tenant, or other occupier.

### Mixed use units plan

Southport is a 'mixed use units plan' according to the [Unit Titles (Management) Act 2011 (ACT)](https://www.legislation.act.gov.au/View/a/2011-41/current/html/2011-41.html), s 39A, because it has both residential and non-residential units. While mainly residential, Southport has three shops. This classification affects the membership of the Executive Committee.

## Other resources

### Owners Corporation Network (OCN ACT)

An association of unit owners and body corporates established to further the interests of those living in medium and high-density housing in the ACT. After an initial joining fee of $5.00, annual membership for individuals is $20. Corporate membership for Southport would cost $600 p.a. The OCN ACT organises information nights and publishes quarterly newsletters and information, including a '[Best Practice Guide](https://www.ocnact.org.au/best-practice-guide)'.

### Strata Community Association (SCA)

The peak body for industry managers, lot owners, tenants, and stakeholders in strata title, body corporate, community title, and owners corporations. Though Southport would be eligible, the SCA is mainly a professional association for strata managers. Benefits include professional development, professional resources, code of conduct, and dispute resolution.

Free downloads from SCA include:

- the 'Australian Building Manual Guideline'
- 'A data-driven holistic understanding of strata insurance in Australia and New Zealand'
- '[Electric Vehicles in Strata](https://www.strata.community/electric-vehicles-in-strata)'.

### Facility Management Association (FMA)

A professional association representing professionals, teams, and organisations involved in the strategic and operational management of facilities in both public and private sectors throughout Australia, plus supporting organisations.

### LookUpStrata

A platform for unit owners and Executive Committees offering informative emails, newsletters, and webinars. It is free but includes advertising. The goal is to promote freedom of knowledge and open conversations in the strata community.

## Trello

### What is Trello?

A simple app for managing projects, available on desktop computers, smartphones, and tablets.

### Origin and purpose

The idea originated from the Building Manager, who wanted to use Trello to manage projects. On 9 December 2024, the EC agreed to utilise Trello for tracking discussion, approvals, and progress on items of major works that occur out-of-session. The Building Manager populated Trello with all current items requiring EC input or being developed for visibility.

A project management app manages projects more effectively than email because it:

- visualises projects and their status
- interactively connects projects, people, and resources
- consolidates documents, discussion, and history
- organises and stores project records
- reduces clutter.

Trello was chosen because it is:

- the Building Manager's choice for his projects
- the Secretary's choice for EC projects
- multi-platform
- powerful
- simple
- visual (Kanban-based).

### How Trello works

Each card represents a project containing all its information and discussion. Each list represents a stage in a project's progress, so cards move from left to right as projects progress. The exception is the "Grady's recommendations for EC approval" list, positioned left-most for easier smartphone access.

### How EC members should use Trello

EC members should check one list on each of two Trello Boards:

- 'Grady's recommendations awaiting EC approval' on Grady's Board called 'Southport'
- 'New and ongoing agenda items' on the Secretary's Board called 'Meeting Board (EC in confidence)'.

For each card on 'Grady's recommendations awaiting EC approval', members should:

- vote for the recommendation
- explain disagreement
- ask Grady for more information.

For each card on 'New and ongoing agenda items', members should read and consider it before the next meeting. Members can also create a card to add an agenda item.

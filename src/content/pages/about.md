## Design and build

![Southport design and build](../../assets/images/about/design-and-build.jpg)

Southport was a project by [GEOCON](https://geocon.com.au), the largest residential developer in Canberra and the fifth-largest in Australia. Eight years on, GEOCON continues to support us, including providing information for this website.

Award-winning [Cox Architecture](https://www.coxarchitecture.com.au) designed the architectural concept, which [Peckvonhartel](https://www.pvh.com.au) then detailed. There are 353 units and three shops (see below).

The 32 'Terrace' units are unique, with each featuring two courtyards (front and rear), two levels, cross-flow ventilation, and adjoining undercover parking.

Southport comprises two buildings:

- Stage 1 is the quadrant facing the lake
- Stage 2 is the tower on Anketell Street.\*

\* *We're considering renaming the buildings more descriptively than the developer's project stages.*

## Amenities

![Swimmer](../../assets/images/about/swimmer.jpg)

Southport's residents are privileged to have access to the following amenities.

### Two pools

Our 25-metre lap pool and smaller 'plunge pool' are in Stage 1, nestled in the sheltered but sunny courtyard garden on Level 1.

Stick around after your swim. There's a sunken seating area, an outdoor table, and comfortable lounges. Soak up the sun and snooze to the soothing sound of our stunning waterfall. Shelter under our shade sail if it's hot.

The pools are 1.2 metres deep, but with shallower ledges for little ones in the plunge pool. They are heated from the Labour Day long weekend in October until the Anzac Day long weekend in April.

![Plunge pool, 25-metre lap pool, and waterfall](../../assets/images/about/pools.png)

*Plunge pool and 25-metre lap pool, with waterfall.*

### Gym

In Stage 1, on Level 5, is our sunny and scenic gym. While working out on our quality [Technogym](https://www.technogym.com/en-AU/) equipment (expertly maintained by Technogym), enjoy the views, greenery, and birdlife — no more dingy basements like many gyms.

There are toilets and showers for when you've finished your workout.

![Southport gym](../../assets/images/about/gym.jpeg)

## Accessibility

We value accessibility.

For example, we have:

- powered doors (more planned)
- ramps
- accessible parking (some units)
- straight-through lifts for wheelchairs and mobility scooters
- tactile ground surface indicators in our pool area.

![Pool hoist](../../assets/images/about/pool-hoist.jpg)

We are considering installing a pool hoist (above). We want everyone to have a great time using our pools.

### Accessibility plus

Southport's accessibility extends beyond its buildings to the surrounding area. The surfaces are flat and paved, intersections are equipped with traffic lights, and the views are invigorating.

We love seeing the smiles of independence as residents head out on their walkers and mobility scooters to access essential services, especially healthcare.

Then, we enjoy watching them return with groceries, medications, or takeaways. It's an accessibility we genuinely value and don't take for granted.

## Environment

You care. So do we.

We thank GEOCON for:

- a central location
- solar-passive design and construction (EER 5+, so your energy bills will delight you).

![Buses pass our door](../../assets/images/about/buses-pass-our-door.jpeg)

*Buses pass our door. You'll drive less.*

Later, we've added:

- solar panels (a 50 kW array)
- motion-activated LED lighting in the carparks
- 'Food Organics and Garden Organics' (FOGO) bins.

We are learning how to improve our energy efficiency and sustainability even more:

- we are one of only seven Canberra apartments involved in the ACT Government's 'Sustainable Apartments Pilot', which will plan our path to full electrification, including EV charging
- we are assisting the ANU's Centre for Energy Systems with their 'ACT Apartment Decarbonisation 2025' project.

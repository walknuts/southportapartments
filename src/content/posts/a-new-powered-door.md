As part of our plan to improve accessibility, another door is now powered.

Inside the 35 Oakden Street entrance of Stage 1, it opens into the ground-level carpark and bin room.

![The new powered door and its card reader at the 35 Oakden Street entrance.](../../assets/images/news/a-new-powered-door/powered-door.jpeg)

More powered doors are planned.

We acknowledge MSR Electrical's assistance with this project.

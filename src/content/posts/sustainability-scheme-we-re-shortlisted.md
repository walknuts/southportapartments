Today, the ACT Government informed us they have shortlisted Southport's bid to take part in their 'Sustainable Apartments Pilot'.

What does that mean?

The ACT Government's Climate Choices website states it 'will provide detailed feasibility studies for the electrification of 7 apartment complexes ... at no cost'. It mainly considers switching our hot water from gas to electricity, but it will also explore EV charging.

Having been shortlisted, we are gathering the requested information to move forward to the next stage. We'll update you in future posts.

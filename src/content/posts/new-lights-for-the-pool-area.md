Have you noticed the new lights in our pool area?

The original housings were corroding, their bulbs were failing, and they were discontinued. So we replaced them all with more durable stainless steel lights, which also match the railings and have more in common with our pergola lights.

We acknowledge the assistance of J2 Electrical & Data with this project.
